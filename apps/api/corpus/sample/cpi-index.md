The CPI measures the change in the price of a fixed basket of goods and services bought by
South African households in urban areas. The reference base is **December 2024 = 100**. Rates
quoted are annual (year-on-year) unless stated otherwise.

## Core inflation

Stats SA's core measure is **CPI excluding food and non-alcoholic beverages, fuel and energy**.
It strips the two most volatile categories so the underlying trend is easier to read. The
**trimmed mean** is published as a second core measure: it removes the items whose price
changes are the largest outliers in a given month.

## How to read the numbers

- **Annual (y/y)** compares the index with the same month a year earlier. This is the figure
  usually reported as "the inflation rate".
- **Monthly (m/m)** compares with the previous month and is seasonally volatile.
- The index can be converted to a rate: `(current index / year-earlier index - 1) * 100`.
  For example, July 2026 vs July 2025: `(107,7 / 103,3 - 1) * 100 = 4,3%`.
